# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsors / Business Owners

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors or Business Owners provide business direction, secure organizational support, and remain accountable for the intended business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business need, expected outcomes, and priority
- Secure or escalate decisions about funding, staffing, and organizational resources
- Make or escalate go/no-go and major scope decisions
- Resolve escalations that are beyond the delivery team's authority

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to align business outcomes, product priorities, and trade-offs
- Work with Project Managers on escalations, resource constraints, and milestone decisions
- Receive progress, risk, and decision updates from the Project Manager and provide timely direction
- Consult Developers and technical leads as needed to understand feasibility and delivery implications

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and go/no-go reviews
- Milestone updates and escalation decisions
- Sponsor or stakeholder briefings

---

## Engineering / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering or Technical Leads guide technical direction and coordinate engineering work so that delivery plans are feasible, secure, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and engineering implementation choices
- Help estimate work and sequence implementation
- Identify technical risks, integration points, and dependencies
- Support engineering quality, maintainability, and technical decision records

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, review, and technical problem-solving
- Work with Product Managers to assess feasibility and explain technical options and trade-offs
- Coordinate with Project Managers on estimates, dependencies, and schedule risks
- Engage QA/Testing and Security/Privacy partners early to plan verification and controls

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs and decision records
- Engineering planning and dependency reviews
- Delivery risk updates and code reviews

---

## UX / Research Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Research Leads ensure that product decisions and delivered experiences are informed by user needs and usability evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Develop interaction designs, prototypes, and usability guidance
- Validate user-facing solutions and identify accessibility or usability concerns
- Share evidence and recommendations that inform product decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to connect user needs and research findings to requirements and priorities
- Work with Developers to clarify designs and resolve implementation questions
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to include relevant user-facing behaviors in acceptance criteria and validation
- Share findings with Project Managers and stakeholders when they affect scope, risks, or milestones

### Typical Communication
- Research plans, findings, and design reviews
- Prototypes and usability validation
- Product planning and acceptance-criteria discussions

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads make success measures actionable by defining how outcomes are measured and validating the resulting evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define metric specifications, instrumentation needs, and evaluation plans
- Help teams identify baselines, targets, and data quality requirements
- Validate that collected data supports reliable outcome measurement
- Analyze results and communicate evidence and limitations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to define success metrics and interpret outcomes
- Coordinate with Developers on instrumentation and event implementation
- Partner with QA/Testing to verify measurement behavior and data quality
- Share analysis with Project Managers and stakeholders to inform status and follow-up decisions

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and measurement plans
- Instrumentation reviews and data-quality checks
- Outcome analysis and stakeholder updates

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness and help ensure that releases can be deployed, monitored, and supported reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release readiness, deployment activities, and operational handoffs
- Confirm monitoring, support, and rollback or mitigation plans are prepared
- Monitor operational signals after release and coordinate response to issues
- Capture operational follow-up actions and reliability improvements

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Developers and QA/Testing on release checks, smoke tests, and deployment readiness
- Work with Project Managers on release timing, dependencies, and stakeholder communications
- Partner with Product Managers and support stakeholders on user impact and post-release follow-up
- Escalate operational risks and incidents through the established incident process

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment plans
- Operational dashboards and post-deployment checks
- Incident updates and release retrospectives

---

## Security / Privacy Partners

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Partners advise teams on security and privacy risks and help ensure appropriate requirements and safeguards are addressed.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements and risks
- Recommend mitigations and review relevant designs or controls
- Advise on verification needs and security/privacy findings
- Help route security or privacy incidents through the established response process

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers and technical leads to design and implement appropriate controls
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy verification
- Work with Product Managers to clarify user, product, and regulatory implications
- Advise Project Managers on risk tracking and escalation, and involve Sponsors when a decision exceeds the team's authority

### Typical Communication
- Risk and design reviews
- Security/privacy requirements and verification findings
- Incident-response coordination and mitigation updates

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Adapt role assignments to the project's size and needs; one person may cover multiple roles, and not every project requires a dedicated person for each role.

# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through a customer-focused, iterative lifecycle. Teams initiate projects by aligning on outcomes and stakeholders, then plan scope, backlog, milestones, and dependencies. During execution, they track delivery and quality, manage risks, and communicate with stakeholders. Releases include verification and rollback readiness, and retrospectives turn delivery insights into continuous improvements. Project and product leads, developers, QA, and stakeholders collaborate with clear ownership throughout.
